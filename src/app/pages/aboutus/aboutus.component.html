Built by someone who experienced the scheduling problem firsthand.

ScheduleUP was created to make academic scheduling simpler, more transparent, and less frustrating for universities. What started as a university project evolved into a platform focused on helping departments manage academic data and generate schedules more efficiently.

Universities spend too much time managing schedules.

Creating a timetable is rarely just placing classes into rooms. Departments have to coordinate professors, student groups, classrooms, course requirements, and many possible conflicts.

Professors and staff

Availability, workload, and overlapping teaching hours.

Student groups

Group schedules, lectures, labs, and study program requirements.

Rooms and spaces

Classroom capacity, room availability, and lab requirements.

Repeated revisions

Manual changes often create new conflicts somewhere else.

Our mission

To help universities spend less time fighting schedules and more time focusing on education.

ScheduleUP aims to reduce repetitive administrative work, make academic data easier to manage, and provide clear schedules for students, professors, and department staff.

From university project to growing platform.

ScheduleUP started as a university project aimed at solving one specific challenge: creating better academic schedules. Since then, it has grown into a platform focused on helping departments manage academic data, reduce scheduling complexity, and provide clearer timetable access for students and staff.

2024

The beginning

Meet the person behind ScheduleUP.

ScheduleUP was created by Adenita Luta, a Computer Science graduate from FSHMN University.

While studying and working with academic schedules, it became clear how much time departments spend maintaining data, resolving conflicts, and adjusting timetables manually.

ScheduleUP was developed to address those challenges through automation, practical design, and modern web technologies.
