About ScheduleUP

Building a smarter way for universities to create schedules.

ScheduleUP was created to simplify university scheduling by helping departments manage academic data, generate optimized schedules, and publish clear timetables for students and staff.

Our purpose

Why ScheduleUP exists

University scheduling is often repetitive, time-consuming, and difficult to keep organized. ScheduleUP aims to make that process faster, clearer, and easier to manage.

Our Vision

To provide universities with a modern scheduling platform that combines automation, clean data management, and simple schedule access in one place.

Our Mission

To reduce manual scheduling work, minimize conflicts, and give students and academic staff a better way to view and follow their schedules.

What we offer

A focused platform for academic scheduling

ScheduleUP is designed around the real scheduling needs of universities and departments.

Automatic Schedule Generation

Generate schedules using optimization logic instead of manually combining classes, rooms, professors, and groups.

Conflict Reduction

Help reduce common scheduling issues such as overlapping professors, unavailable rooms, or clashing student groups.

Clean Schedule Views

Let users find schedules in a simple interface without exposing private department data.

Future Integrations

ScheduleUP is planned to support features such as Google Calendar synchronization for easier schedule tracking.

Developer and Founder

A project built from real scheduling problems.

ScheduleUP was developed by Adenita Luta, a Computer Science graduate from FSHMN University. The project started with the goal of making university scheduling less manual, less confusing, and more accessible for everyone involved.

From data management to schedule generation and user-friendly timetable views, ScheduleUP reflects a practical approach to solving a complex academic process.
